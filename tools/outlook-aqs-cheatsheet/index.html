Outlook AQS • Offline cheatsheet

Find it. Bulk it. Clear it.

Practical Outlook search recipes for inbox cleanup, meeting noise, newsletters, attachments, people, dates and more precise combinations. Click any query to copy it into Outlook search.

The inbox-cleanup trick

Unread meeting requests you already handled in Calendar

kind:meetings AND read:no

Search the mailbox, review the results, then bulk mark as read or remove stale invitations.

Unread meeting items

Meetings

Find unread appointments and meeting requests in one go.

kind:meetings AND read:no

Older meeting noise

Meetings

Meeting items received last month. Useful before bulk cleanup.

kind:meetings AND received:"last month"

Newsletter candidates

Cleanup

Find messages whose body contains the common unsubscribe marker.

body:unsubscribe
Heuristic: transactional mail can also contain “unsubscribe”, so review before deleting.

Recent newsletter candidates

Cleanup

Reduce the noise to unsubscribe-style mail from this month.

body:unsubscribe AND received:"this month"

Newsletter-like subjects

Cleanup

Search common newsletter wording in the subject.

subject:(newsletter OR digest OR "weekly update")
Adapt the words to patterns you actually see in your mailbox.

Messages where you were CC’d

Cleanup

Focus on mail where your address or name appears on the Cc line.

cc:"your name"

Everything from one sender

People

Use an email address for the cleanest match.

from:person@company.com

Sender + exact topic

People

Messages from one person that mention an exact phrase anywhere Outlook searches.

from:person@company.com AND "project phoenix"

Either of two senders

People

Combine alternatives using uppercase OR.

from:alice@company.com OR from:bob@company.com

Exact phrase in subject

Content

Quotes force the words to appear together in that order.

subject:"status report"

Both keywords in the body

Content

Require multiple concepts to be present in the message body.

body:(project AND budget)

Either keyword in the body

Content

Useful when the same issue is described with different wording.

body:(error OR failed OR failure)

Topic, minus unwanted noise

Content

Find a topic while excluding a recurring subject pattern.

"project phoenix" NOT subject:"automated notification"

Sender + topic anywhere

Content

about: can match the phrase in subject, body, or attachment contents.

from:"Alex Smith" AND about:"status report"

Anything with attachments

Files

A fast way to isolate likely large or document-heavy mail.

hasattachment:yes

Specific attachment name

Files

Search attachment names, or matches inside supported attachment contents.

attachments:presentation.pptx

Files

to:Recipient

about:Subject, body or supported attachment content

Status & files
